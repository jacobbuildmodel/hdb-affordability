TWO VINTAGE ANOMALIES, recorded rather than resolved:

  1. The Resale Price Index page reports a last-updated date of 28 August 2024, but
     the file contains observations through 2026-Q2. A 2024 vintage cannot contain
     2026 data, so either the displayed date refers to the dataset metadata rather
     than the data, or the date was misread. The RPI is used for context only and
     no published figure depends on it, but this needs settling before the article
     quotes any RPI number.
  2. income_resident_vs_employed.csv stops at 2023, which IS consistent with its
     28 October 2024 vintage even though the filename says 2000-2025. Not an error.

NOT OBTAINED, and the hunt is now closed: the Additional CPF Housing Grant income
ceiling. Three routes were tried and all failed. The Wayback Machine has no snapshot
of HDB's AHG page. HDB's press release archive does not reach back to September 2019.
The 2011 flyer's table stops at 2,250 dollars a month because that was the SHG
ceiling, not the AHG's. A party website gives 5,000 but a party website is not a
source for a number on this site.

CONSEQUENCE, stated in the article rather than worked around: the grant-adjusted
measures run from 2019 to 2025 while the conventional measure runs from 2013 to
2025. No AHG amount is modelled for any year.

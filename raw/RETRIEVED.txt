      METHOD NOTE, recorded because the first attempt produced a false alarm.
      Reading this document in text order does not work. On pages 2 and 4 the
      quarter labels are emitted AFTER their value rows and out of sequence, so
      a naive "label, then the next percentage" parse silently mismatched 44 of
      109 rows and reported Jan-Mar 2010 as a deviation. It was not one. The
      rows were re-read by geometry instead, anchoring on each label's y-centre
      and taking the first percentage to its right within an 8-point band, which
      resolves all 109 rows with none left unparsed. One row (Oct-Dec 2016) sits
      0.84 points below its label and needs that tolerance; a tighter band drops
      it and it too reads 2.50%.

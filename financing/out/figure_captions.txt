Chart 2. Realised break-even spread by loan start year, R2, with a band showing the range across 3, 12, 24 and 36-month reset intervals (AMENDMENT 3). The 2024 and 2025 markers are dimmed: under 36 months realised. Pre-2020 starts are shaded: they were priced off SIBOR and are quoted here over SORA, which overstates their break-even spread.

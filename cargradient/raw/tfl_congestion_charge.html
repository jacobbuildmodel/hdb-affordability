Congestion Charge

The Congestion Charge is an £18 daily charge (if paid on the day or in advance) or £21 (if paid by midnight of the third day after travel).

You must pay the daily charge, if you drive within the Congestion Charge zone 7:00-18:00 Monday-Friday and 12:00-18:00 Sat-Sun and bank holidays. No charge between Christmas Day and New Year's Day bank holiday (inclusive).

The easiest way to pay is by setting up Auto Pay. Exemptions and discounts are also available.

If your vehicle does not meet the Ultra Low Emission Zone (ULEZ) standards, you must also pay the ULEZ charge.
